Journey

Enjoy music on a forest train journey and color the forest with any melody you make. Make a sound with Arduino and Adafruit Circuit playground to change the color of trees in the forest. Make other interactions with the photoresistor and a button.


Equiptment: Arduino Nano, Adafruit Circuit playground, PC/Projector, Audio
Software: Processing
